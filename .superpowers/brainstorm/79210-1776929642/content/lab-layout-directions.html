Workspace-First Portal

Feels closest to a membership portal from the first click. Best for direct-user flow, but weaker for SEO/GEO discovery and product storytelling.

Recommendation

I recommend A. SEO-Led Product Flow with a friendlier portal interior inspired by your reference image.

Why it fits

  • Best for SEO and GEO because the landing page emphasizes systems, outcomes, and structured content.
  • Keeps the workspace approachable instead of overly operational.
  • Separates discovery from implementation cleanly: public narrative outside, guided portal inside.

Tradeoff

  • The "Open Workspace" action becomes secondary on the public page.
  • The app should still feel premium enough that it does not read like a downgraded course area.
