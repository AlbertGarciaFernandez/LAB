# SEO URL Architecture

## Positioning

CodeHunter Lab should not behave like a business that only sells in the Netherlands, and it should not behave like a business with no local presence either.

Recommended positioning:

- Based in Leiden, Netherlands
- Working across Europe and internationally

That lets the site keep local trust signals while still targeting broader English-language commercial demand.

## Keep as Netherlands

These pages match local or location-shaped demand and should stay geo-specific:

- `/en/ai-automation-consulting-netherlands`
- `/en/dental-clinic-automation-netherlands`
- `/en/physiotherapy-clinic-automation-netherlands`
- `/en/aesthetic-clinic-automation-netherlands`
- `/en/veterinary-clinic-automation-netherlands`
- `/en/accounting-firm-automation-netherlands`
- `/en/real-estate-automation-netherlands`
- `/en/software-development-leiden`

## Do not build yet

Avoid these until stronger authority and conversion data exist:

- More city pages in the Netherlands with near-duplicate content
- Dutch-language service pages that cannot be written and maintained at native quality
- Generic informational articles such as `what is ai`
- Europe or global duplicates of existing Netherlands pages without a different search intent
